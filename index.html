Science Shorts Uploader

Science Shorts Uploader is an internal tool operated by a single individual. It uploads original, self-produced short-form science videos to one YouTube channel owned by the operator.

This application uses YouTube API Services. Every video is reviewed and manually approved by the operator before it is uploaded. The tool has no public interface, no other users, and does not access any YouTube account other than the operator's own.
